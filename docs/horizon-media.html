Horizon OS Media Experience

Lead prototyper for the Media Watch Experience on Horizon OS — shipped the HzTV app at Connect 2025 as the central media hub.

Horizon OS Unity 3D C# VR / MR Shipped Product

Overview

Lead prototyper for the Media Watch Experience on Horizon OS, driving the HzTV app and an immersive cinema effort from ambiguous concept to a clear, leadership-backed product direction.

What Shipped: HzTV at Connect 2025

I led HzTV app implementation and its interactive experience end-to-end — shipping at Connect 2025 with core year-end features, and advancing “TV” as the central media hub. The work targeted deeper user engagement and new monetization opportunities.

The Approach: Design in Working Code

I designed and built the interactive UX in working code — animations, landing page, scrolling, 2.5D treatments, and transitions — so the experience could be felt, not just reviewed on a screen. To get there, I delivered 45 production builds in three months and built in-headset prototypes that powered weekly playtesting, turning open questions into go/no-go decisions and shaping both design and leadership direction.

Beyond Proof of Concept

My prototypes didn't stop at proof of concept — they became a working reference for the engineering team, directly shaping how features were implemented and helping engineers align on interaction details and technical approach before development began, cutting down on rework.

Because the prototypes were built in real, running code, they bridged the gap between design intent and engineering execution — letting design judgment and engineering craft validate and reinforce each other.

Forward Foundation

Beyond the build, my prototype insights fed future product strategy and laid the foundation for the next wave of immersive-media features — making development more agile and faster to market.
