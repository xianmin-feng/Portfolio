About Me

At Meta, I lead end-to-end prototyping for Horizon OS in Unity 3D and C# — shipping core UI like the System Bar, Navigator, and Settings to production, and delivering director- and executive-level demos that shaped product direction.

My work lives at the intersection of design and engineering. I don't just design screens — I build them in working code, so teams can feel a concept and make real go/no-go calls before anything ships. That same approach carries beyond Meta: AI-native iOS apps in SwiftUI like Nibi and Cattō, and React web prototypes — real applications where design judgment and engineering craft compound.

I also help grow the craft across the team — mentoring designers and junior prototypers, leading cross-functional Unity workshops, and interviewing to build out the AR/VR org.
