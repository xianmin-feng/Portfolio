100 Days of Sketching

A daily art challenge — 100 consecutive days of sketching and digital painting in Procreate. The discipline that builds the eye behind every product decision.

Procreate iPad Daily Practice Personal Project

Why I Did This

Visual fundamentals don't show up only on a canvas — they show up in every product decision. Composition becomes layout. Color theory becomes hierarchy. Light and shadow become depth and elevation. Character expression becomes microcopy and tone. I committed to 100 consecutive days of sketching to keep these instincts sharp and let them feed the work I do as a designer.

The Work

A selection from the 100 days — characters, compositions, color studies, and quiet moments.

What It Taught Me

The same discipline that makes a single sketch feel alive is the discipline that makes a product feel cared-for. Composition becomes spacing. Color theory becomes accent palettes. The decision to keep a brushstroke or push it further is the same decision you make on every UI detail. 100 days of daily drawing didn't just sharpen my hand — it sharpened the eye I bring to every interaction I design.
